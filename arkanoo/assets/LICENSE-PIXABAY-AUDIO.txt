The prepared WAV files preserve the original game's SDL-decoded stereo content,
converted from the original 44,100 Hz mixer format to the host's 48,000 Hz PCM
format. No trimming, normalization or looping was applied. They accompany the
game adaptation and are not offered as a standalone sound library.

The original source files, their hashes, creators and conversion reference are
listed in the repository's recorded-effects fixture and CREDITS.md. Players
need no conversion tool or MP3 decoder. Other sound effects and music are not
covered by this notice.
